# AI Chat Report

The AI Chat Report feature allows admins to ask questions and instantly get insights related to users, workouts, activity, and overall platform performance.

--- 

## Access AI Chat Report

1. Go to the left panel.
1. Under Analytics, click on AI Chat Report.
1. The AI Chat Report page will open.

![Treatment plan page](/img/AIChat.webp)

---

## Ask Questions

On the page, you can:
- Select from pre-created questions, or

![Treatment plan page](/img/AIChat4.webp)

- Type your own question in the input box. Example 
  - How many active users are there? 
  - What is the total number of workouts performed?
  - What is my score based on the last workout I performed?

![Treatment plan page](/img/AIChat1.webp)

--- 

## View AI Report

1. Submit your question.
2. The AI chat Report will display the answer on the screen.
3. You can continue asking more questions to get different insights.
