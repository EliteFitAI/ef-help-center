# Navbar settings

## Language Change

The portal allows you to change the microsite language easily.
1. On the Dashboard, go to the top header.
1. You will see the Language Change option beside the notification bell icon.
1. Click on it to view the list of available languages.
1. Select your preferred language.

![Treatment plan page](/img/Lang.webp)

![Treatment plan page](/img/Lang1.webp)

Once selected, the microsite will automatically change to the chosen language.

---

## Dark Mode & Light Mode

You can switch between dark and light modes for better viewing.
1. On the top header, click the crescent moon icon.
1. The portal will switch to Dark Mode.

![Treatment plan page](/img/Dark.webp)

3. Click the icon again (it will change to a sun icon).
3. The portal will switch back to Light Mode.

![Treatment plan page](/img/Light.webp)

This helps customize the portal based on your viewing preference.
